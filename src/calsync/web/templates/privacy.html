CalSync

Privacy Policy

CalSync stores appointment details only so it can create, update, list, and cancel shared family calendar entries on your behalf.

What we keep

Appointment titles, dates, times, notes, locations, and local audit records for the shared scheduling workspace.

What we do not sell

CalSync does not sell appointment data, ad profiles, or household scheduling history.

Why the data exists

The service keeps a local record so conversational channels like the web console, ChatGPT, and Alexa can act on the same calendar brain.

Retention and access

  • Calendar writes stay scoped

    Apple calendar credentials remain on the origin service and are not copied into the Cloudflare Worker.

  • Voice requests are verified

    Alexa requests must pass Amazon signature verification and match an approved skill ID before CalSync processes them.

  • Need changes or deletion?

    Contact the household operator for this deployment to rotate tokens, remove appointment records, or disable connected channels.
